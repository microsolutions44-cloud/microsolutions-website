Common Problems We Fix Daily

Not sure what's wrong with your device? Here are some of the most common issues we solve.

Logo Looping (Boot Loop)

Stuck on the Apple logo or restarting continuously.

Sudden Restarts

Phone repeatedly shutting off every 3 to 5 minutes.

Not Turning On

Dead devices showing no sign of power or charge.

Screen Cracks & Lines

Broken glass, black spots, flickering screens, or color lines.

Overheating Issues

Device runs excessively hot during normal daily use.

Loose, Corroded Ports

Charging cable feels loose, doesn't stay in, or needs cleaning.

Camera Malfunctions

Blurry photos, shaking lenses, or black camera screens.

Water & Liquid Exposure

Accidental spills or submersion requiring deep cleaning.
